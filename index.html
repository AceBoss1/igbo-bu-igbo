Who We Are

Preserving Igbo Identity for Generations

Igbo Bu Igbo is a registered cultural and unity initiative dedicated to uniting Igbo people through cultural preservation, community development, and collective empowerment — wherever they may be across Nigeria and the world.

Cultural Preservation

Safeguarding Igbo language, traditions, arts, and values

Youth Empowerment

Programs and scholarships for the next generation

Community Welfare

Support systems and welfare for members in need

Global Unity

Connecting Igbo across all states and the diaspora

Igbo Speaking States in Nigeria
Core Igbo Homeland
Membership comprises Igbo people in the traditional Igbo-speaking states — the heartland of Igbo culture, language, and identity.
7 Chapters
These 7 chapters cover the five South-East states — the ancestral Igbo homeland — plus Delta and Rivers states, which have significant indigenous Igbo-speaking communities.

Non-Igbo Speaking States in Nigeria
Igbo Diaspora Within Nigeria
Membership comprises Igbo people residing in other Nigerian states and FCT Abuja — Igbo who have settled, worked, or lived outside the homeland.
30 Chapters
These 30 chapters cover the Federal Capital Territory and all 29 non-Igbo-speaking states. Igbo communities in these states maintain strong cultural ties and active chapter programmes.
