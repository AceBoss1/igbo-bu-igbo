Frontend

Next.js 14 App Router — SSR for SEO, API routes for backend, React components, Tailwind CSS. Deployed on Vercel free tier. Separate dashboard, admin, and public routes.

Backend + Database

Firebase Firestore for all data, Firebase Auth for all users, Firebase Storage for photos/docs. No separate server at launch — Firebase handles everything.

Payments

Paystack — all NGN transactions (registration, dues, wallet deposits, marketplace). 1.5% + ₦100 per transaction, capped at ₦2,000. Zero monthly fee.

Marketplace

Built on Firestore collections with Firebase Storage for product images. No third-party marketplace service needed at launch — fully custom-built.

Storage runs out faster than expected with marketplace images. At Phase 5 launch: 43 chapters × ~50 product listings × 3 images × 300 KB = ~1.8 GiB for marketplace alone. Add member photos (~1 GiB), food sharing item photos (~200 MB), and bulk purchase product images (~200 MB) and you hit the 5 GiB free limit with only ~2,000–3,000 active marketplace listings. Integrate Cloudinary at Phase 5 start — before public marketplace launch — not at "scale phase".
Recommended image storage strategy: Use Firebase Storage for member photos and ID documents (1–2 GiB). Route all marketplace product images, bulk purchase item photos, and food sharing images through Cloudinary free tier (25 GiB storage, 25 GiB bandwidth/month — far more generous than Firebase). Cloudinary also auto-generates WebP thumbnails, resizes on-the-fly, and delivers via global CDN for faster load on Nigerian mobile networks.

Funds are held in the group wallet (a Firestore balance, not a bank account) and only released to the coordinator in stages, with admin sign-off at each gate.

5
Supplier Directory & International Purchase Preparation
The /suppliers collection builds a verified, rated directory of international and local suppliers that coordinators can reuse across multiple bulk purchase groups. This prevents fraudulent supplier submissions and builds institutional memory.

International Suppliers

Primarily China (electronics, fashion, machinery). Key considerations: customs duties (must be calculated and included in the per-slot price upfront), lead time (sea = 45–60 days, air = 7–14 days), payment (Western Union, Wise, or Alipay — coordinator handles). Include estimated duty cost in slot price calculation.

Local Suppliers

Nigerian market (Onitsha Main Market, Aba, Kano). Payment via bank transfer with Paystack invoice. Lead time: 2–5 days. No customs. Coordinator visits in person or uses verified agent. Food items primarily sourced from local wholesale markets.

Slot Price Formula

Slot price = (Unit cost × 1 slot) + Shipping per unit + Customs per unit + IBI coordination fee (3–5%). Display the breakdown transparently in the group listing so members understand what they're paying for.
