IBI Main — Your Member Account & Identification Number

IBI Main — Member Account and Identification Number

Every IBI member is assigned a unique IBI Main — their Member Account and Identification Number — structured as a chapter prefix followed by a 10-digit unique identifier in line with the NUBAN (Nigerian Uniform Bank Account Number) standard.

Numbers are not issued sequentially — each number is randomly generated and unique, so your membership number cannot be guessed or reverse-engineered. This also means that when Igbo Bu Igbo becomes a licensed financial institution, your IBI Wallet account number and your membership number remain identical — no migration needed.

Future-proof design: Your IBI Main is your IBI Wallet number today and your bank account number tomorrow — One identity. One number. Forever.
